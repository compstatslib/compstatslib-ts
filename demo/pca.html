Interactive PCA

Click in the plot to add points. From the third point, the two principal component arrows appear — PC1 solid, PC2 dotted, each as long as the spread it explains — and turn with every point after it. This is interactive_pca() from the R package.

The bundled pca_degenerate dataset (16 points, exported from the R package) spreads almost equally in two directions: its arrows come out nearly the same length, so the first component is barely more "principal" than the second.
