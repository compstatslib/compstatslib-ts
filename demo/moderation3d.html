Interactive 3D moderation

The surface is the fitted moderated regression y ~ x * z over the bundled moderation_data; its twist is the interaction. Drag to rotate freely, or use the Z-rotation slider: 0 aligns the IV slope plane with the screen, 270 the moderator slope plane. "Include control w" refits with the noise predictor w in the model, held at its mean over the surface. This is interactive_moderation_3d() from the R package.
