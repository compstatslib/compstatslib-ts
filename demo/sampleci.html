Sample confidence intervals

Each row is one sample of 100 from a simulated population: its 99% interval in the light color, its 95% interval over that, and a diamond at its mean. Rows whose interval misses the true mean (the vertical line) are drawn in coral — about 5 in every 100. Draw again simulates a fresh population; the page holds one seeded generator, so reloading it replays the same sequence of pictures. This is plot_sample_ci() from the R package.
